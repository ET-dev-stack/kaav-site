About KAAV

KAAV is Erik Torstensson Boije & Friends product development studio. We design and build physical products, software tools, and interactive experiences — spanning live-performance visual technology, hardware experiments, and playful, tactile tools. Our design process is based on quick prototypes and playful iteration all the way to release.

The best thing in life is taking ideas from prototype to shipped brand and products, staying involved with what we have built long after launch.
