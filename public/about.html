About KAAV

KAAV is a Swedish product development studio. We design and build physical products, software tools, and interactive experiences — spanning live-performance visual technology, hardware experiments, and playful, tactile tools.

We work in short, focused cycles: taking ideas from prototype to shipped product, and staying involved with what we build long after launch.
